Levers

From data: lanes and staff follow gate_activity.csv for each 15-min interval. Custom: you set staff per gate, and staff are conserved: adding someone to a gate takes one from another gate (one with a half-staffed lane first, otherwise the least busy), and removing someone sends them to the busiest gate. The 60 never changes, and a gate can't hold more people than it has lane positions; lanes = staff ÷ staff-per-lane, capped at the gate's max lanes. Closing a gate sends its arrivals to the two nearest open gates (60/40). Scanner downtime removes lanes while it lasts.
